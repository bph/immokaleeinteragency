The Immokalee Interagency Council (IIAC) is an umbrella organization, counting social services, governmental agencies and social business among its members.

The IIAC meets every second Wednesday of the month at Immokalee Community Park, 321 N 1st Street, Immokalee, FL 34142. (Map)

Please connect with any of the people to receive information about upcoming meetings, or subscribe to the Listserv below.
